# Section 3 - Methods
## 3.1 Geometric Formulation
### 3.1.1 Representation of Phase Space in $\operatorname{Cl}_{2,0}(\mathbb{R}^2)$
- **Manifold Mapping:** A mapping from traditional Kuramoto phases $\theta_i\in\mathbb{S}^1$ to rotors 
  $R_i = \cos{(\theta_i/2)} - I\sin{(\theta_i/2)}$, where $I=\mathbf{e}_1\mathbf{e}_2$ satisfies $I^2=-1$.
- **Coordinate-Free Invariance:** Formal proof establishing why updating states via direct geometric multiplication maps
  onto the underlying curved phase manifold cleanly, eliminating the structural coordinate dependencies found in 
  standard Euclidean solvers.
### 3.1.2 Dynamic Vector Field and Lie Algebra Operations
- **Intrinsic Frequency Generators:** Formulation of uncoupled natural drift terms as fixed bivector elements 
  $\Omega_i = \omega_i I$.
- **Network Coupling Field:** Vectorized representation of the network topology coupling vector field over arbitrary
  graphs. Mapping the phase-interaction term into an asymmetric bivector field generated by the rotor 
  cross-multiplication matrix.
## 3.2 Numerical Methods
### 3.2.1 Invariant Lie-Group Runge-Kutta Munthe-Kass (RKMK) Scheme
- **Tangent Space Integration:** Explanation of integrating trajectories strictly inside the tangent space (bivector 
  space) relative to an instantaneous basepoint $y_n$ rather than performing flat Euclidean operations.
- **Sequential Stage Trajectories:** Step-by-step description of the sequential Runge-Kutta updates, showing how
  intermediate vector fields are evaluated sequentially to prevent order reduction on curved surfaces.
- **Exponential Mapping:** Projecting the accumulated bivector step back onto the Lie group manifold using the exact 
  exponential map, ensuring perfect preservation of the normalization constraint ($R_i\tilde{R}_i=1$) without numerical
  projection steps.
### 3.2.2 Validation Framework and Metric Foundations
- **Control Baseline:** Verification mechanics for the decoupled system ($K=0$) ensuring total energy conservation ($H$)
  and zero phase space volume contraction $(\Lambda(t)=0$).
- **Symmetry Check:** Verification protocol checking absolute trajectory invariance under global $\operatorname{SO}(2)$
  bivector transformations.
- **Time-Reversibility Standard:** Comparative formulation of the time-reversal verification metric, highlighting why
  tracking symplectic structure through geometric inversion ($-\Delta t$) prevents the phase desynchronization seen in
  standard adaptive routines.
